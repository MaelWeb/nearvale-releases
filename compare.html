Nearvale vs ProximityLock vs Unlock

Compared on 2026-11-26

Several Mac apps lock your screen when you walk away with a Bluetooth device. This page compares Nearvale with two well-known ones, ProximityLock and Unlock – Modern Proximity Lock. We make Nearvale, so we have tried to stick to what each product says about itself and to say plainly where the others do something we do not.

Which one fits you

  • Pick ProximityLock if you want automatic unlocking, need to use an Android phone or another Bluetooth device, or want to use it on many Macs.
  • Pick Unlock if you prefer an App Store purchase and are happy to run its iPhone app as part of the setup.
  • Pick Nearvale if you wear an Apple Watch, do not want to install anything on your phone or watch, and prefer a lower price. It adds a calibration wizard, one-tap false-lock feedback that retunes the thresholds, trusted places, and a pause while a call or presentation keeps the display awake.

Whichever you choose, treat it as a convenience: it measures Bluetooth signal strength, not distance, and it is not a substitute for a strong password and FileVault.
