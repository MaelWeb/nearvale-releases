Questions

What do I need?
A Mac with Bluetooth running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, and an Apple Watch. For hands-free unlocking, turn on “Unlock with Apple Watch” in System Settings.
Do I need an app on my watch?
No. Nearvale reads the Bluetooth signal your watch already sends.
Is this a security product?
No — it is a convenience tool. Bluetooth can be blocked or lost, so keep a strong password and FileVault on, and lock manually when it matters.
What happens when the trial ends?
Automatic locking stops and the app tells you so clearly. Your settings are kept; enter a license key and everything resumes.
What is the difference between the subscription and lifetime?
None in features; both work on up to 3 Macs at a time. Lifetime is a single payment. The annual subscription renews each year; if you cancel, automatic locking stops when the paid year ends. If a subscription ends, a renewal fails or a purchase is refunded, the app tells you clearly and stops auto-locking, and it resumes on its own once the renewal is paid.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Open the customer portal, enter the email you bought with, and sign in from the link we email you. There you can cancel, change your card or see your renewal date. Settings → License in the app also has a “Manage Subscription…” button.
Can I move to a new Mac?
Yes. Choose “Deactivate This Mac” in Settings → License, then activate on the new one. You can use up to 3 Macs at the same time.
