It stops only if the service explicitly reports the key as invalid (for example after a refund, when a subscription ends, or when a renewal payment fails), or if it has been unable to check for more than 30 days. 6. Safety notice

Bluetooth signals can be blocked, delayed, spoofed or lost; macOS changes can break detection; and some features you choose to enable (for example trusted places, backup devices, or deferring locks during meetings) intentionally prevent or delay locking. We do not guarantee that your Mac will lock at any particular time, or at all. You remain responsible for protecting your Mac and the information on it, for example by using a strong password, FileVault, and locking it manually when it matters.
