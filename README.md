# Nearvale

**Walk away. Your Mac locks itself.**

Nearvale is a macOS menu-bar app that locks your Mac when your Apple Watch moves away, and wakes the display when you come back. Nothing to install on the watch, no account, no analytics.

![An illustration of Nearvale: the watch walks away, the Mac locks, the watch returns and the screen wakes](https://nearvale.app/assets/demo-en.gif)

*An illustration of the flow, not a screen recording.*

## What it does

- **Locks when you leave, wakes when you return.** Uses the Bluetooth signal your Apple Watch already sends.
- **Calibrates to your desk.** A ten-second wizard sets the thresholds for your room, your Mac and your watch.
- **Learns from false locks.** Mark a lock as a mistake and the thresholds relax.
- **Gets out of your way.** Trusted places (by network or by a spot on the map), a pause while a call or presentation keeps the display awake, a backup device such as an iPhone for when the watch briefly drops out.
- **Private by design.** The trial makes no network requests. After purchase the app only contacts the payment provider to activate and re-check the license. No analytics, no crash reporting; your password is never stored or sent.
