# Nearvale

Walk away, and your Mac locks itself. Nearvale is a macOS menu-bar app that locks your Mac when your Apple Watch moves away and wakes it when you come back.

- **Download:** [latest release](https://github.com/MaelWeb/nearvale-releases/releases/latest) — universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 14 or later, with a 14-day free trial.
- **Website and help:** <https://nearvale.app>
- **Report a problem:** [open an issue](https://github.com/MaelWeb/nearvale-releases/issues) and attach the log exported from Settings → Diagnostics (it is redacted). Never post your license key here — for license or refund questions, email support@nearvale.app.

This repository hosts releases, the website and the issue tracker; the source code is not public. Use of Nearvale is governed by the [License Agreement](https://nearvale.app/terms.html). Versions released as WatchLock up to v1.1.6 remain under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.
