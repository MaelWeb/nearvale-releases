Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Nearvale comes with a 14-day free trial, so you can make sure it works with your Mac and your watch before you pay. If it still isn't right for you, you can get your money back.

14-day refund, no questions asked

  • Lifetime license: request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase, for any reason.
  • Annual subscription: request a full refund within 14 days of the first charge, and within 14 days of each yearly renewal charge, for any reason. If your subscription starts with a free trial at checkout, nothing is charged during the trial, so there is nothing to refund.

How to request a refund

  • Email support@nearvale.app from the address you used to buy, and include your order number or license key; or
  • use the link in the receipt email from Dodo Payments.

Refunds are issued by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, to the original payment method. How long the money takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

What happens to your license

When a purchase is refunded, its license key is disabled. Refunding a subscription charge also ends the subscription. Nearvale stops running with that key after its next online check, and automatic locking stops. Your settings stay on your Mac.

After 14 days

Refunds after 14 days are not guaranteed, but contact us if something is wrong and we will try to help. This policy does not limit any rights you have under the consumer-protection law of the country where you live.

Cancelling a subscription

The lifetime license is a one-time purchase; there is nothing to cancel and you will not be charged again.

You can cancel the annual subscription at any time in the customer portal: enter the email you bought with and sign in from the link we email you. You can also reach it from Settings → License → Manage Subscription… in the app. After you cancel, you are not charged again and Nearvale keeps working until the end of the year you have paid for. Cancelling during a free trial at checkout means you are never charged.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; we can usually resolve it faster. A disputed or reversed payment disables the related license key.
